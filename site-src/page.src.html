Opening it the first time

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"
  1. That's because TODOPOP is new and isn't signed by Microsoft yet. Nothing is wrong with your download.
  2. Click More info, then Run anyway. TODOPOP installs and opens.
  3. You'll only see this once.
Mac says TODOPOP "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it
  1. Open the downloaded TODOPOP-mac.dmg and drag TODOPOP into Applications.
  2. Open TODOPOP from Applications. If macOS blocks it, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to TODOPOP. Confirm with your password.
  4. TODOPOP lives in the menu bar at the top of your screen, not the Dock. You'll only need to do this once.
Android asks before installing TODOPOP
  1. Open the downloaded TODOPOP-android.apk, from the notification or your Downloads.
  2. If Android says your browser isn't allowed to install apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details → Install anyway. It's new, so Google hasn't seen it yet.
