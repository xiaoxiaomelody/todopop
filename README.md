# TODOPOP

## Your to-do list, but impossible to ignore

TODOPOP turns unfinished tasks into colorful bubbles that float over your screen and
grow with time. The longer a task waits, the harder it is to miss—until you finish
it, pop it, and get that tiny burst of satisfaction.

It is a playful focus companion for the tasks you keep postponing: lightweight
enough to stay out of your way, visible enough to keep your priorities in sight,
and tactile enough to make finishing feel rewarding. Use it on your Mac or Windows
desktop, then pair your phone as a remote when you are away from your desk.

### What makes TODOPOP different?

- **Tasks become visible.** Your to-dos float above the desktop instead of
  disappearing into a forgotten list.
- **Urgency has a shape.** Bubbles grow as tasks wait, giving your attention a
  gentle nudge without turning your day into a wall of notifications.
- **Completion feels physical.** Pop a finished task and clear a little space in
  your head.
- **Desktop plus phone.** Keep the main experience on your computer and use your
  phone to add, edit, pause, or pop tasks remotely.
- **Private by design.** Pairing the phone and computer does not require handing
  over an email address or phone number.

TODOPOP is free to try and is being built in the open. If you have a task that
keeps escaping every productivity system, give it a bubble—and see what happens.
